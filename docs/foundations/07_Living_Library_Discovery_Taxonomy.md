# Foundation 7: The Living Library Discovery Taxonomy

## Purpose

Traditional library catalogs are designed to organize collections.

The MFEE Living Library is designed to help people discover books.

This distinction shapes every aspect of the Living Library.

Rather than asking only, *"Where does this book belong?"*, the Living Library also asks:

- *Who might love this book?*
- *When might someone need this book?*
- *What conversations could this book inspire?*
- *How might this book support learning?*
- *What experiences does this book reflect?*

The Discovery Taxonomy provides the shared language that allows these questions to be answered consistently.

---

# Discovery Before Classification

Traditional cataloging systems primarily support retrieval.

The Living Library supports discovery.

Classification remains important, but it is only one dimension of understanding a book.

Every book can be discovered through many different pathways.

A child may search for a story about friendship.

A teacher may search for books that support a science lesson.

A parent may look for books that reflect their family's experiences.

A tutor may search for engaging read-alouds.

Each search is equally valid.

The Living Library is designed to support them all.

---

# Multiple Paths Into Every Book

Every Book Profile may include multiple dimensions of discovery.

These dimensions work together rather than competing with one another.

Examples include:

- Themes
- Subjects
- Instructional Supports
- Representation
- Community Voices
- Reader Recommendations
- Reading Experiences

Together they create a richer understanding of each book than traditional catalog records alone.

---

# Discovery Is Relational

Books do not exist in isolation.

Readers do not discover books in the same way.

The Living Library recognizes that discovery happens through relationships.

Relationships between:

- books
- readers
- educators
- families
- tutors
- experiences
- ideas
- communities

Every connection makes future discovery easier.

---

# A Living Vocabulary

The Discovery Taxonomy is intentionally designed to evolve.

As the collection grows, new terms, perspectives, and experiences may be added.

The taxonomy should remain:

- consistent
- inclusive
- human-centered
- educationally meaningful
- community-informed

Growth should improve discovery without creating unnecessary complexity.

---

# Principles for New Vocabulary

Every new term added to the Living Library should satisfy three questions.

## 1. Does it help someone discover a book?

If not, it probably does not belong in the taxonomy.

## 2. Is it understandable without specialized training?

The Living Library serves educators, families, volunteers, and students.

Its language should remain accessible.

## 3. Does it strengthen relationships?

The Living Library exists to connect people with books and with one another.

Vocabulary should always support that mission.

---

# The Goal

The goal of the Discovery Taxonomy is not to describe every possible characteristic of a book.

The goal is to help every reader find the right book at the right moment.

When discovery becomes easier, reading becomes more joyful, relationships become stronger, and the library becomes a living part of the community.

That is the purpose of the MFEE Living Library.
