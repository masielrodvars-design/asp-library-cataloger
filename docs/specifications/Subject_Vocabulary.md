# Purpose

The Subject Vocabulary describes what a book is about.

Unlike themes, which describe enduring ideas and human experiences, subjects describe the people, places, things, events, and topics that readers encounter within a book.

Subjects support curiosity-driven discovery.

Children often begin their search with a topic they are interested in. Teachers frequently search by instructional topic. Families may look for books connected to a child's interests.

The purpose of the Subject Vocabulary is to make those books easy to find.

---

# Guiding Principles

Subjects should be:

- Familiar
- Concrete
- Easy to understand
- Useful for discovery
- Appropriate for children and families

Whenever possible, subjects should describe observable topics rather than abstract ideas.

# Vocabulary Governance

The Subject Vocabulary is expected to grow over time.

New subjects should be added only when they improve discovery and represent recurring interests within the Living Library collection.

The goal is to maintain a vocabulary that is both comprehensive and approachable.

---

# Relationship to the Living Library

Subjects describe what readers will encounter.

Themes describe what readers may take away.

Together they create a richer understanding of every book.
