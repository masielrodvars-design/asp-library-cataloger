# Living Library Book Story Template

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Purpose:** Defines the standard reader experience for every book in the Living Library.

---

# Philosophy

A Book Story is not a catalog record.

It is an invitation to discover a book.

Every section should help readers, families, tutors, educators, and volunteers connect more deeply with the book and with one another.

---

# Book Story Structure

## 1. Basic Information

- Title
- Author
- Cover Image
- Language(s)
- Series (if applicable)
- Genre / Format
- Age Range

---

## 2. Why This Book Matters

A welcoming description that answers:

> Why might someone love this book?

This is not a plot summary.

It is an invitation to read.

---

## 3. Themes

The enduring ideas readers may take away from the story.

---

## 4. Subjects

The people, places, ideas, or topics readers encounter.

---

## 5. Great For...

Examples include:

- Family Reading
- Read Aloud
- Independent Reading
- Classroom Discussion
- Social-Emotional Learning
- STEM Learning

---

## 6. Curated Collections

Community-created collections that include this book.

---

## 7. Community Voices

Recommendations, reflections, and stories from readers, tutors, educators, families, and volunteers.

---

## 8. Perfect Moments

This book is especially meaningful when...

Examples:

- Starting school
- Learning to make friends
- Building confidence
- Exploring nature
- Talking about grief
- Encouraging creativity

---

## 9. Library Learning

Help readers find the book while learning how libraries work.

Includes:

- Section
- Call Number
- Shelf Location
- Library Tip

---

## 10. Conversation Starter

One thoughtful question that encourages discussion.

---

## 11. Accessibility

Languages Available

Reading Experiences

- Full Story
- Quick Overview
- Beginning Reader
- Audio (future)

---

## 12. Tutor Mode (Future)

Discussion prompts

Extension activities

Vocabulary

Related books

---

# Guiding Principle

Every section should strengthen at least one relationship:

- reader ↔ book
- child ↔ family
- tutor ↔ student
- educator ↔ learner
- community ↔ reading
