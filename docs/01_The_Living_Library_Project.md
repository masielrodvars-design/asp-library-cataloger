### Building a relationship-centered learning ecosystem that connects the right book with the right learner at the right moment.

Relationships between children and stories.

Between older students and younger readers.

Between families and shared reading.

Between teachers and ideas.

Between tutors and learners.

Between volunteers and the community they serve.

Between one generation of readers and the next.

# Why We Exist

The MFEE Learning Center Library is more than a place where books are stored.

It is more than a catalog.

It is more than a collection.

It is a **relationship-centered learning ecosystem** where children, families, educators, tutors, volunteers, librarians, and community members connect through books.

Every day, relationships are formed around stories.

A teacher discovers the perfect read-aloud.

A tutor finds a book that helps a struggling reader gain confidence.

A parent and child share a favorite bedtime story.

An older student recommends a book that inspires a younger reader.

A volunteer helps build a collection that serves an entire community.

These moments are the heart of the library.

The Living Library Project exists to make those moments easier to discover, strengthen, share, and preserve.

---

# Relationships Are Our Foundation

Everything MFEE does begins with relationships.

Relationships between schools and families.

Relationships between teachers and students.

Relationships between volunteers and children.

Relationships between community members who believe every child deserves the opportunity to thrive.

The MFEE Learning Center Library is another expression of that same philosophy.

Books become the way those relationships begin, deepen, and continue across generations.

Our library is not simply organized around books.

It is organized around the people whose lives are connected through them.

---

# A Community Collection

Every library has books.

Our library has stories behind the books.

The MFEE Learning Center Library has been built through the generosity of families, volunteers, educators, and community members who have shared books that were once part of their own homes.

Many books on our shelves were bedtime favorites.

Some helped children learn to read.

Some comforted them during difficult moments.

Some made them laugh until they cried.

When those books are donated to the Learning Center, they begin a new chapter.

They become part of another child's story.

Every donated book represents an act of generosity.

Every borrowed book represents an opportunity.

Every recommendation represents an act of mentorship.

Together, they transform a collection of books into a community resource.

---

# Every Book Has Many Stories

Traditional library catalogs describe books.

The Living Library tells the stories that grow around them.

Every book has the story written by its author.

But it also has the story of the child who first loved it.

The family who donated it.

The teacher who built a lesson around it.

The tutor who used it to unlock a difficult concept.

The volunteer who recommended it.

The parent who read it every night.

The eighth grader who encouraged a second grader to give it a chance.

These stories matter.

They help the next reader discover not only a book, but also the experiences of the community that surrounds it.

The Living Library exists to preserve those stories.

---

# Every Reader Becomes a Contributor

Libraries have always preserved books.

The Living Library also preserves wisdom.

Every member of our community has something valuable to contribute.

A teacher can share how a book transformed a classroom discussion.

A tutor can recommend a title that helped a multilingual learner gain confidence.

A parent can explain why a story became a family favorite.

A volunteer can suggest the perfect book for a hesitant reader.

A student can simply say,

> *"I think you'll love this book."*

Those recommendations become gifts to future readers.

Every reader leaves the library richer than they found it.

The library becomes richer because of every reader.

---

# Why Artificial Intelligence Belongs Here

Artificial intelligence is not the heart of this project.

People are.

AI exists to support the community—not replace it.

Its role is to organize knowledge, reveal connections, improve discovery, and help every member of the community find the books that best meet their needs.

The wisdom, compassion, generosity, and lived experiences of our readers will always be more valuable than any algorithm.

The Living Library Project combines the strengths of people and technology so that each can do what it does best.

AI organizes.

People inspire.

Where younger readers search for books recommended by older students they admire.

Where teen tutors become reading mentors.

Where teachers discover new instructional possibilities.

Where tutors share what works.

Where families contribute their stories.

Where volunteers continue giving long after they donate a favorite book.

Where books connect generations.

That is our vision.
