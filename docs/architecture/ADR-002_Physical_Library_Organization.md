# ADR-002: Physical Library Organization

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** August 2026

---

# Context

The MFEE Learning Center seeks to accomplish two educational goals through its physical library.

1. Help children develop transferable library literacy skills that prepare them to confidently use public and school libraries.

2. Create a welcoming environment that encourages curiosity, discovery, and joyful reading.

A question arose during the design of the Living Library:

Should books be organized using traditional library systems or thematic collections?

---

# Decision

The Living Library will use both approaches.

Permanent shelving will follow familiar library conventions whenever practical.

Examples include:

- Picture Books
- Early Readers
- Graphic Novels
- Chapter Books
- Young Adult
- Dewey Decimal classification for nonfiction

This organization helps children develop library literacy and prepares them to navigate libraries beyond the Learning Center.

Alongside the permanent organization, the Learning Center will feature rotating curated collections that invite readers to discover books through themes, ideas, and community recommendations.

Examples include:

- Dream Big
- Friendship & Belonging
- Staff Picks
- Summer Adventures
- Hispanic Heritage Month
- Girls Who Break Barriers

Curated collections may exist both physically within the Learning Center and digitally within the Living Library platform.

---

# Rationale

Traditional organization and curated discovery serve different educational purposes.

Traditional organization teaches children how libraries work.

Curated collections encourage exploration, conversation, and joyful discovery.

Using both approaches creates a bridge between the Learning Center and the Montclair Public Library, allowing students to build transferable library skills while experiencing books in engaging new ways.

---

# Consequences

This decision allows a single book to participate in multiple discovery experiences without changing its permanent location.

For example, a picture book may remain shelved within the Picture Book collection while simultaneously appearing in one or more curated collections.

This approach reinforces the Living Library's philosophy that books can be discovered through many different pathways while maintaining a familiar and accessible physical organization.
